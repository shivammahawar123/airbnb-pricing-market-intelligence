# Dashboard Notes

## Dashboard Overview

The Airbnb Pricing & Market Intelligence Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze Airbnb listings, pricing patterns, demand trends, host performance, and city-level benchmarks across 10 major global cities.

The dashboard enables business users to explore market opportunities, understand pricing behavior, evaluate customer demand, and assess host quality through a unified analytical experience.

---

## Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of four analytical pages:

### 1. Market Intelligence Overview

Purpose:

* Analyze overall market size and city-level performance.
* Compare listings, hosts, and pricing across cities.

KPIs:

* Median Price
* Total Listings
* Total Neighbourhoods
* Total Cities
* Total Hosts

Visuals:

* Listings by City
* Hosts by City
* Listings Share by City
* Median Price by City
* City Benchmark Matrix

---

### 2. Pricing Intelligence

Purpose:

* Identify pricing drivers and premium market segments.
* Analyze room types, property types, and accommodation characteristics.

KPIs:

* Average Price
* Most Expensive Room Type
* Premium Listings %
* Highest Priced City
* Most Expensive Property Type

Visuals:

* Price by Room Type
* Average Price by Property Type
* Average Price by City
* Price Share by Room Type
* Price Impact of Bedrooms

---

### 3. Demand Intelligence

Purpose:

* Analyze customer demand patterns and review activity.
* Understand review distribution and seasonal demand behavior.

KPIs:

* Total Reviews
* Total Reviewers
* Average Reviews per Listing
* Average Rating
* Highest Rated City

Visuals:

* Reviews Trend Over Time
* Top Property Types by Reviews
* Review Share by Room Type
* Reviews by City (Pareto Analysis)
* Monthly Review Seasonality Heatmap

---

### 4. Host & Value Intelligence

Purpose:

* Evaluate host quality, responsiveness, trust indicators, and customer value.

KPIs:

* Superhost %
* Verified Host %
* Average Response Rate
* Average Acceptance Rate
* Best Value City

Visuals:

* City Ratings Benchmark Matrix
* Host Response Rate by City
* Top Cities by Host Acceptance Rate
* Host Verification Status
* Superhost Distribution

---

## Global Slicers

The following slicers are synchronized across all dashboard pages:

* City
* Room Type
* Property Type
* Instant Booking

---

## Key Features

* Multi-page business-focused dashboard architecture.
* Interactive cross-filtering and drill-down analysis.
* Consistent KPI framework across all pages.
* City benchmarking and comparative analytics.
* Pricing, demand, host, and market intelligence in a single report.
* Professional Airbnb-themed visual design.
* Executive-friendly layout optimized for business decision-making.
