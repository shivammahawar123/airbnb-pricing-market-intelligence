# Airbnb Pricing & Market Intelligence

## Business Report

### Executive Summary

This project analyzes Airbnb listings, hosts, and customer reviews across ten major global cities to understand market structure, pricing behavior, customer demand, host performance, and traveler value.

Using SQL, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, and Power BI, the project transforms raw Airbnb data into actionable business intelligence that can support hosts, travelers, and hospitality stakeholders in making data-driven decisions.

---

# Business Context

The global Airbnb marketplace consists of diverse cities, property types, host profiles, and customer behaviors. Understanding the factors that influence pricing, demand, and customer satisfaction is essential for improving market performance and identifying growth opportunities.

This project was designed to evaluate Airbnb markets through four business domains:

* Market Intelligence
* Pricing Intelligence
* Demand Intelligence
* Host Intelligence

---

# Business Objectives

The analysis focused on the following objectives:

* Compare Airbnb market characteristics across major global cities.
* Identify key factors influencing listing prices.
* Evaluate pricing differences across room types and property types.
* Analyze customer review activity and seasonality trends.
* Assess host performance and customer satisfaction.
* Explore value-for-travel opportunities across cities.

To support these objectives, a KPI framework was developed covering market size, pricing, demand, and host quality metrics.

---

# Key Findings

## Market Intelligence

Paris emerged as the largest Airbnb market with over 64,000 listings and the highest host participation. Significant differences were observed across cities in terms of listing supply, market maturity, and neighbourhood-level pricing.

Premium neighbourhoods were concentrated in cities such as Cape Town, Bangkok, Rio de Janeiro, and Mexico City.

## Pricing Intelligence

Property characteristics were identified as the strongest drivers of price.

Key pricing drivers included:

* Number of bedrooms
* Accommodation capacity
* Property type
* Room type

Entire villas achieved the highest average prices, while larger properties consistently commanded substantial pricing premiums.

## Demand Intelligence

Customer review activity increased steadily from 2008 to 2019, indicating strong marketplace growth.

Demand exhibited clear seasonal patterns, with review activity peaking between September and January. October recorded the highest review volume across the dataset.

Rome demonstrated the highest customer engagement, achieving the greatest average reviews per listing.

## Host Intelligence

Host quality was strongly associated with customer satisfaction.

Superhosts consistently achieved:

* Higher review ratings
* Higher average prices
* Stronger marketplace performance

Response rate also showed a positive relationship with customer ratings, indicating that responsiveness is an important contributor to guest experience.

## Value Assessment

A Value Index was developed to compare customer satisfaction relative to listing prices.

Rome, Paris, and New York delivered the strongest balance between affordability and customer satisfaction, while higher-priced destinations such as Cape Town and Bangkok produced lower value scores despite strong ratings.

Due to multi-currency pricing across countries, value comparisons should be interpreted as directional indicators rather than direct financial comparisons.

---

# Business Recommendations

### For Hosts

* Improve response rates to enhance guest satisfaction.
* Pursue Superhost status to strengthen pricing power and trust.
* Optimize pricing strategies using property size and accommodation capacity.

### For Travelers

* Consider Rome, Paris, and New York for strong value-for-money experiences.
* Use seasonal demand patterns when planning travel and bookings.

### For Business Stakeholders

* Monitor high-growth markets and premium neighbourhoods for expansion opportunities.
* Track host quality metrics as leading indicators of customer satisfaction.
* Use KPI-driven dashboards to support ongoing market monitoring.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that Airbnb market performance varies significantly across cities, property types, and host segments.

Pricing is primarily driven by property characteristics, customer demand follows clear seasonal patterns, and host responsiveness contributes positively to guest satisfaction. The resulting dashboard and KPI framework provide a scalable foundation for monitoring market performance and supporting data-driven decision-making within the short-term rental industry.
